# Code of Conduct

This project is open to everyone who wants to use it, report problems, or
contribute, in Arabic or English.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with new users.
- Keep discussion on the app and the problem being solved.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.

## Not accepted

- Insults, harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination of any kind.
- Sharing other people's private information.
- Posting or requesting games, ROMs, BIOS files, disc images, keys, firmware,
  Redump data, or links to them.
- Spam and repeated off-topic posts.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock issues and pull
requests, and block users who break these rules.

To report a problem with someone's behaviour, contact the maintainer privately
through GitHub. Reports are kept confidential.

## Scope

These rules apply to issues, pull requests, discussions, the wiki, and any
other project space on GitHub.
