# iso2god-rs notice

CHD Tool includes iso2god-rs so it can convert Xbox 360 ISOs to Games on Demand
(GOD), which Xenia and an Xbox 360 can load.

## Included tool

- Tool: iso2god.exe (published as `iso2god-x86_64-windows.exe`)
- Project: iso2god-rs by Ilia Pozdnyakov
- Version: 1.8.1
- License: MIT (`Tools\iso2god\win-x64\LICENSE.txt`)
- Source: https://github.com/iliazeus/iso2god-rs/tree/v1.8.1
- Path: Tools\iso2god\win-x64
- SHA-256: 1459FCF8FF5A89E039B02C68C06B006B7A036F3B1CA237C2D7E03D61BD211D47

## What it is used for

- convert an Xbox 360 ISO to Games on Demand

It never writes to or deletes the source. The app checks the SHA-256 above
before every run.
